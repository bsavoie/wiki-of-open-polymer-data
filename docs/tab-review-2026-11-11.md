# Overview, Applications, and Degradation review — 2026-11-11

The qualitative pass read these three tabs across all 262 polymer entries, using the polyethylene family, carboxymethyl cellulose, and polythiophene pilot entries as depth benchmarks. Reviewers considered material-specific explanations, applications, exposure mechanisms and outcomes, the scope of each source, and the limits of environmental and recycling evidence. A sentence-count screen was also used as a formatting check, not as a substitute for reading the content.

The accompanying [follow-up list](tab-review-followups-2026-11-11.csv) preserves 79 reviewer-identified research questions on tabs whose text was not changed in this pass. These are candidates for further investigation, not findings that a tab is unsupported or that the literature contains no applicable study. Some concern an evidence gap that the entry already identifies, such as an unmeasured natural-environment lifetime. A targeted search did not establish usable evidence for several candidates; others require a deeper search, independent source verification, or reconciliation with a related material's scope. The list does not purport to enumerate every open scientific question in the corpus.

Changes within a tab can resolve one question while leaving others open. In particular, environmental biodegradation, service aging, controlled chemical recycling, additive migration, and physical disintegration measure different things. A laboratory result for a grade, blend, particle size, or exposure should not be generalized to an entire family. Where a source disagreed with itself, the text either qualifies the result or leaves the disputed figures for further review.
