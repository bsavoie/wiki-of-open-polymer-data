# Review of Overview, Applications and Degradation enrichment

Reviewed 2026-11-11 against the approved polyethylene family (`wop-000001`), carboxymethyl cellulose (`wop-000200`) and polythiophene (`wop-000244`) examples. The reviewed authoritative v2 snapshot had SHA-256 `f04c44562a6164005bc84ac04685e6c0a3528251e6464002aa7f7e8b4c5e1c80`. This is an editorial review and screening inventory, not a new source-by-source validation of the entire corpus. No polymer article content was changed during this review.

## Findings

The corpus is **not consistently at the depth of the pilots**. The per-entry [screening inventory](tab-enrichment-review-2026-11-11.csv) covers all 262 records and records the topic-level flags for a future pass.

- **Applications:** 54 distinct entries have a one-sentence subsection: 35 Purchasing and 19 Scale and evidence limits. No Products and industries subsection was one sentence. The flagged texts were individually inspected. A longer Products paragraph can still lack a concrete reason for the use, as with synthetic cis-1,4-polyisoprene (`wop-000135`). Poly(vinyl formal) (`wop-000029`) has a substantive product example but a one-sentence Purchasing subsection. Alkyd (`wop-000125`) is a useful example of the desired Applications depth.
- **Degradation:** 88 distinct entries have a one-sentence labeled topic: one pathways, 34 timescales and 77 environmental persistence. These categories overlap. The flagged paragraphs were individually inspected. No nonempty *whole* recycling subsection is only one sentence, but 43 individual recycling paragraphs are single sentences within longer sections. Phthalonitrile (`wop-000130`) has an empty recycling subsection. Alkyd's environmental-persistence topic remains one sentence; polyethylene's Degradation tab is a useful strong comparison.
- **Overview:** All 262 records have at least two sentences in each of the six rendered prose fields, and the screening found no one-sentence prose paragraphs in those fields. This does not establish comparable depth. For example, alkyd's Properties paragraph says that oil type affects drying and flexibility without explaining the direction or an example. Syndiotactic polypropylene (`wop-000011`) similarly mentions orientation affecting response without an example. The Overview screen identifies 212 entries with at least one of structure, properties and variability under 75 words; these are candidates for editorial review, **not 212 confirmed failures**. Twelve diverse entries were manually reviewed for content; the entire Overview corpus was screened mechanically, not individually judged or re-researched.

Across Applications and the three labeled degradation topics, 108 distinct records have at least one single-sentence subsection; 34 records appear in both groups. These counts do not capture all qualitative holdouts and do not imply that unflagged entries meet the target. Structural depiction captions and scope notes were excluded from the Overview prose screen.

## How to use the inventory

`applications_single_sentence_topics` and `degradation_single_sentence_topics` name the relevant headings. `recycling_empty` identifies an actual empty section; `recycling_single_sentence_paragraphs` counts individual short paragraphs rather than whole subsections. `overview_short_screen_fields` uses a triage threshold of 75 words for structure, properties and variability, and 50 words for overview, nomenclature and health/safety. Thresholds were chosen to find candidates against the richer pilot passages; they are not a required length or an evidence-quality judgment. Sentence counts use punctuation screening with manual verification of flagged Applications and Degradation text; unusual formatting can require reassessment.

The next research pass should inspect each flag in context, seek material-specific evidence, and add concrete mechanisms, directional comparisons, or bounded examples where supported. A genuine evidence gap should remain honestly labeled; an unsupported sentence or invented number is not an acceptable way to satisfy a length rule. Review remaining longer passages for explanatory depth and source applicability as well.
