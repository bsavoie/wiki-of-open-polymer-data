# Historical structure records

WARNING: These are historical, superseded chemical structures from the public version deployed on 10 November 2026. They may be scientifically incorrect, incomplete, or ambiguous. Do not use them as current scientific references.

The external registry records each file's SHA-256, reason for withdrawal or historical status, and a successor identifier where established. A successor is a corrected or current reference, not an assertion that the records are interchangeable. These files are preserved for reproducibility only.
